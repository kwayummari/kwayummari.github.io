Privacy Policy for Lantern Path

Last updated: 6 November 2026

AURORAWAVE LABS ("we", "us") makes the mobile game Lantern Path (the "App"). This policy explains what information is collected when you use the App, and why.

Contact: developerkwayu@gmail.com

1. Summary

  • We don't ask for your name, email, or any account.
  • Your game progress (levels, stars, lamp oil, unlocked styles, settings) is stored only on your device.
  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers and usage data to show and measure ads, as described below.
  • If you're in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, or in a US state with a privacy law, you're asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options.

2. Information stored on your device

The App saves the levels you have finished and their stars, your lamp oil, the lantern styles and light colours you have unlocked, which Daily Lantern puzzles you have solved, and your settings (sound, vibration) in your device's local storage. We don't receive this data. Uninstalling the App deletes it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd., to show banner, interstitial and rewarded video ads. To do this, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID or the iOS Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA, only with your permission on iOS)
  • IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location
  • Device and app information: model, operating system, language, app version
  • Ad interaction data: which ads were shown and whether they were tapped
  • Diagnostic data: crash and performance information about the ad SDK

Google uses this information to show ads (personalized or non-personalized, depending on your choice), limit how often ads repeat, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud.

Learn more:

  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Ads personalization settings: https://adssettings.google.com

Cookies and similar technologies

On mobile, the advertising ID works much like a cookie: Google's SDK uses it, and similar local storage, to remember your ad preferences and measure ads.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA/UK/Switzerland and regulated US states): on first launch a consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) lets you accept or refuse personalized ads. You can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy options.
  • Android: reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads).
  • iOS: you can refuse tracking when asked, or change it later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • If you refuse consent, the App may show non-personalized ads, or none.

4. Children

The App is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and isn't directed at children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, contact us and we'll help remove it.

5. Data sharing and selling

We don't sell your personal information. The only third party that receives data from the App is Google, through AdMob, as described above.

6. Security and retention

We don't store any of your data on our servers. Google keeps advertising data according to its own policies.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your personal data. Because we don't hold your data, most requests are best handled through the controls above or by contacting Google. You can also contact us at developerkwayu@gmail.com.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version. Significant changes will be announced in the App's store listing.
