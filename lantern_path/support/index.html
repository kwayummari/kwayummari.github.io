How to play

Tap a path tile to turn it. Carry the lantern's light along the paths until every house glows. Coloured lanterns only light houses of their own colour, and their light can't pass through another colour. Bridges let two paths cross, and locked tiles can't be turned.

Common questions

How do stars work? Finish a level in fewer moves to earn more stars. Using a hint caps the level at two stars.

I ran out of moves. Each level has a move limit. When you reach it, you can get 10 more moves by watching an optional video or spending lamp oil, or start the level again.

How do I get lamp oil? Finish levels, earn new stars, solve the Daily Lantern, or watch an optional video after a level to double your reward.

Where is my progress saved? On your device only. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

How do I turn off sound or vibration? Home → Settings (gear icon).

How do I change my ad privacy choices? Settings → Privacy options (shown where required). On iPhone you can also change tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
