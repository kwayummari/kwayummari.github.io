How to play

Drag the coral pieces from the tray onto the 8×8 reef. Fill a whole row or column to clear it. Clear again within three moves to ride the current and multiply your points. The run ends when none of your pieces fit.

Common questions

Where is my progress saved? On your device only. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

How do I get pearls? Earn 1 pearl for every 25 points, claim the daily gift, or watch an optional ad in the Reef Shop.

How do I turn off sound or vibration? Home → Settings (gear icon).

How do I change my ad privacy choices? Settings → Privacy options (shown where required). On iPhone you can also change tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
